Sentence Passed (pon Three Men
Convicted of Burglary,
BOYDTON, VA. April 19.—(Special)—
The trial of Dudley Peace and Nim Webb
came off yesterday in the County Court
of Mecklenburg, These men were charged
with being accessories to the robbery of
the depot of the Southern railroad at
Chase City, and also with firing upon
Mr. Nelms, the depot agent, in his depot
one night last winter, Gus Ford was con
victed of both crimes soon after it oc
curred, and sent to the penitentiary for
fifteen vears. Smarting under the severe
punishment the jury inflicted upon him,
the prisoner sent for Mr. Goode, the At
torney for the Commonwealth, and told
that officer that he was unwilling to bear
all the blame for the crime, while there
were others equally as guilty as he, and
gave the names of Dudley Peace and Nim
Webb as his associates, Not more than
two weeks since Ford acted like a mad
man, and it was only after being told
by Mr. Goode, the Attorney for the Com
monwealth, that he would have him sent
at once to the penitentiary that he was
brought to his senses. When placed on
the stand he gave a clear account of the
robbery, and testified positivély as to the
guilt of the two men,
Judge Homes, immediately after the
trial of the last two prisoners, proceeded
to pass sentence upon the three men—
Gus Ford for fifteen years, and Peace
and Webb for thirteen years each.
