Five thousand spectators saw the opening of the
New-York Trotting Association's first meeting at

Horsemen of prominence from Maine to California
in the crowd, and all were delighted with

WESTERN WOMEN EAGER TO ORGANIZE
SEPARATE CHAMPIONSHIP.
It is possible that before the women's national
championship takes place at Baltusrol the Western
Golf Association may enter the field with a cham
pionship for the women of the West. Heretofore,
in the absence of such a tournament, the title of
Western weman champion has been conceded to the
winner of the Governors’ Cup in the Onwentsia Golf
Club's tournament. But with the rise in importance
of other Western clubs the winners of similar
trophies at Glen View or Exmoor might with equal
propriety lay claim to the same title. This being
the case, Western golfers are of the opinion that
the time has come for an official award of the
wemen's championship, such as is ade at the
metropolitan championships of this city.

Three so-called championships are now held
annually in the West for women, namely, the com
petition for the Governors’ Cup at Onwentsia, the
“championship gold medal’ at Wheaton and the
women’s cup at Glen View, but none is recognized
as definitely determining the title. According to
these at the head of the United States Golf Asso
ciation, the national championships have reached a
pent where some limit must be placed on the
entries, and the fairest way of determining this
seems to be by accepting only the players who have
shown their fitness in preliminary sectional tourna
ments. The metropolitan ehampionship operates in
this way in bringing éut the best talent of the
East, and the proposed Western championship
would, in their opinicn, do the necessary weeding
out at the West,
Members of the boayd of directors of the West
ern Golf Association ‘say that it is too late to
establish such a tournament this season, but as the
need of such a contest has been amply demon
strated it is probable that they will designate
one of the clubs yet to hold its annual women's
tournament as the one to confer the championship
title. Reports from various leading clubs in the
Chicago district indicate that such action would
receive cordial support, and that the outside entry
would be ampie to make the contest a representa
tive one.
The move is being watched with keen interest
by players in and about New-York, as the tourna
ment, if held. will offer the first real indication of
the kind of opposition that may be expected at
Baltusrol, and more particularly the rivals that
Miss Frances Griscom. the present champion, will
be called upon to meet.
