Plain Truths Well Told.
Buffalo (N. Y.) Sunday Truth, Labor
Organ: The workingmen have got over the
scare that "tariff reform" means less work
and reduction of wages. They know that
it means more work and more purchasable
value for the money earned. They know
that it means less blood money for usurers
and monopolists. They know that it
means cheaper rent, cheaper homes and
more of them; more opportunities for the
unemployed and better times all around.
"Truth was cautious in taking its posi
tion in this campaign. We desire to do
right by our constituents. We desire to
mislead no one. Our interests were and are
bound up in the welfare of the working
element of the country. And now we say,
midway in this most vital campaign, that
a careful study of the question of tariff re
form, and contact with the intelligent labor
ing element of the country, has convinced
us—and assured us of the conviction of
leading workingmen—that a reduction of
the war taxes on the necessaries of life is
absolutely necessary to the advancement
of labor and labor organizations."
