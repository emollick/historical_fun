----- VERBATIM TEXT (National Intelligencer, 13 Nov. 1809, p. 3) -----

So many concurring accounts reach us of the death of Meriwether Lewis, Esq. Governor of Louisiana, by suicide, that we can no longer refuse giving credit to it. His death appears to have taken place on the night of the 10th ult. at the house of Mr. Grinder, near the Indian line in Tennessee. Every one acquainted with his worth will feel a deep regret at this unfortunate event.

----- NOTE -----
The phrase "near the Indian line" is the Nashville Clarion's (20 Oct. 1809; see sources/1809-10-31_kentucky_gazette_reprint_of_clarion_20oct1809.md), which suggests the Intelligencer's "concurring accounts" were the western newspaper reprints. The Intelligencer's 15 Nov. 1809 issue (pp. 2-3) was also captured and contains no further mention of Lewis.
