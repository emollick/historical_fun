# Henry Livingston Jr. verse corpus - MANIFEST

Built from the Wayback Machine captures (2024-25) of Mary S. Van Deusen's site henrylivingston.com. One file per poem, header block then text; one verse line per text line, blank line between stanzas; site spelling and punctuation retained (no modernization); editorial queries, footnote markers, stanza numerals and headnotes removed from the bodies (headnotes are quoted in the notes field).

Attribution scale (mapped from the site's own tiers): **certain** = in Livingston's own hand (manuscript poetry book, daughter Jane's book, loose autograph drafts, tombstone) or signed with his name; **probable** = signed "R" in the Poughkeepsie Journal / New-York Magazine / New-York Weekly Museum in the years when "R" is documented as Livingston, or attributed by descendants / family ownership of the printed copy; **doubtful** = attributed by Van Deusen on style alone (unsigned carrier addresses etc.), or signed R/other pseudonyms in other periodicals or late.

The disputed poem itself ("Account of a Visit from St. Nicholas", Troy Sentinel 1823) is stored separately in `disputed/` and is NOT counted in the totals below.

## Problems and caveats

- Site line counts vs this corpus: the site's "All Henry Poetry" table gives per-poem line counts; this corpus matches them except: Joanna's 33rd year (site 16, corpus 24: the site counted only the first MS page), Belle (site 14, corpus 16: final couplet), Pakepsy (site 41, corpus 40), New-York rebus (98 vs 99 incl. the solution), Hannibal rebus + solution (35 vs 36), Receipt for Writing Novels (41 vs 42: the site's second page of this poem, novels.htm, has one more line than receiptforwritingnovels.htm), Tipple and Smoke (site 32 counts the abbreviated refrain "Delighting, &c." as four lines; corpus keeps the text as printed, 23 lines), Original Poems fragment (12 vs 13; one wholly illegible line dropped).
- Wrapped lines: the site breaks some long anapestic lines across two HTML lines; these were re-joined (deity, apollo, hero, war, sages, alcmena, careless, pakepsy, tappen). Counts were verified against the site's totals.
- Two versions: where the site gives both a manuscript and a printed text (Timmy, Vine and Oak, Epithalamium, Frontier Song, Deity rebus, Rispah, Careless Philosopher, To Miss/Roses, Montgomery parody), the MS text is kept and the printed variant is noted. For A Fair Bargain the Poughkeepsie Journal text is kept, but the page also shows an earlier "Columbian Magazine" printing, which makes Livingston's authorship unlikely. For Tears of Science the family-papers text is kept, not the 1857 Yale variant.
- Illegible words are marked "x"/"xx"/"[xx]" in the site transcriptions (Original Poems, Verses of the Post 1787, PJ Owner, 1805 and 1811 carrier addresses); kept as is. Editorial insertion "[t']" in Easter kept.
- The 1788 New Year's Piece is a prose dramatic scene with one 14-line verse speech; only the verse is kept.
- Dates for undated MS-book poems are approximated from their position in the manuscript book (c. 1784-89); the numbering is only approximately chronological.
- Hope (Ladies' Museum 1817, signed R) is filed as doubtful although signed R, following the site's own lower ranking; the two 1794 acrostics and Past is the Hour (signed R, NY Weekly Museum) are filed as probable following the site's "assumed by pseudonym R" tier.
- The by-source page links "heron.htm" for the Hero Rebus (404); the working page is hero.htm. The pages ca1788/ca1807/ca1811/ca1815/ca1816/ca1823.htm duplicate the carrieraddresses/ pages and were ignored.
- Google Books and HathiTrust could not be used (403 to automated fetches); the American Magazine 1788 and the Weekly Museum are not on archive.org, so Deity rebus (printed version), Robin, Rispah, Past is the Hour, the acrostics, Hannibal, Cant Phrases, On the New-Year and Affectation could not be cross-checked independently.
- Visit from St. Nicholas: the site lists it among Livingston's poems (tier "attributed by descendants' statements or by ownership"); it is stored under disputed/ and excluded from all totals.
