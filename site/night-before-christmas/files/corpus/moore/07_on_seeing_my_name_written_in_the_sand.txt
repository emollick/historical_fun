title: Lines on Seeing My Name Written by a Young Lady in the Sand of the Sea-Shore
author: Clement Clarke Moore
date: pub. 1844
source: Poems (New York: Bartlett & Welford, 1844), pp. 88-88; scan https://archive.org/details/poemsmoor00moorrich (leaves n101 ff.)
form: iambic tetrameter/trimeter quatrains (common measure, abab)
lines: 8
attribution: certain
notes: Light epigram; 8 lines; 'Flora'. Heading as printed: 'LINES / ON SEEING MY NAME WRITTEN BY A YOUNG LADY IN THE SAND OF / THE SEA-SHORE.'.

This name here drawn by Flora's hand
Portrays, alas! her mind:
The beating surf and yielding sand
Soon leave no trace behind.

But Flora's name shall still abide
In many a bosom trac'd,
Not e'en by time's destroying tide
Nor fortune's storms effac'd.
