# Dyatlov Pass — conflicts in the forensic record

Companion to `case-file-forensic-facts.md` (fact numbers F… refer to it). Three kinds of conflict are listed: **A.** the dyatlovpass.com English translation vs the Russian transcription, where the difference matters; **B.** contradictions between forensic documents (Acts vs histology vs testimony vs closing resolution vs volume 2); **C.** internal gaps and oddities within a single document, plus corrections to the working brief. Russian is the controlling text throughout.

## A. English translation vs Russian original (material differences)

## C. Internal gaps and oddities

| # | Item | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Examiners of the Slobodin autopsy (8 Mar 1959) | The Slobodin Act names only Vozrozhdenny as examiner (with Klinov, Ivanov and witnesses Gordo, Naskichev present). Laptev co-signed only the four 4 March Acts. The Dyatlov Act's signature block lacks the witnesses' signatures although they are named in the header. |
| C2 | "Cap of ice" for Slobodin | No ice, ice bed or ice crust appears in any autopsy or forensic document; that detail belongs to the search/scene documents. |
| C3 | "Whether Dubinina could have walked" | No such question is in the interrogation protocol; the nearest answer is "She could have been conscious … a person with a heart wound … talks, runs, asks for help" (F190). |
| C4 | Kolevatov "no significant injuries" | The phrase is the closing resolution's («не имеет телесных повреждений»), not the Act's; see B3. |
| C5 | "6-8 hours after last meal" | Given for all five March bodies with identical wording despite stomach contents ranging from "traces" (Kolmogorova, Krivonischenko) to ~150 cm³ (Doroshenko). Not given for the ravine four. |
| C6 | Slobodin's fracture and "in life" | The Act never states in terms that the fracture was inflicted in life; it says the injury "undoubtedly caused short-term stunning" and that the absence of a clearly expressed subarachnoid/subdural hemorrhage supports freezing as the cause of death, while separately recording 75 cm³ of bloody fluid under the dura and diffuse temporal-muscle hemorrhages. The generic sentence "injuries were inflicted in life, in the agonal state and post-mortem" is the only vitality statement. |
| C7 | Slobodin's pleural effusion | "Up to 1 litre of bloody fluid /effusion/" in the pleural cavities is recorded without comment; the other four March bodies have free pleural cavities. |
| C8 | Krivonischenko's scalp hemorrhage | «в правой височной области и затылочной области разлитое кровоизлияние с пропитыванием правой височной мышцы» (L.116) is an in-life head-impact finding that is not mentioned in the conclusion, which lists only abrasions, skin wounds and burns. |
| C9 | Dubinina's bladder | Not recorded — the only Act without a bladder finding; her heart is given as «12 х 4 х 5 см» (almost certainly a typo). |
| C10 | Thibeaux's kidney | «9 х 6 х 6 см» (right) is implausible next to 10 x 6 x 3 (left); typo. Kolmogorova's heart «12 х 10,6 см» likewise. |
| C11 | Table 2, row 4 | The item "from №2" (Zolotaryov) has no legible name in the scan or the transcription. |
| C12 | Radiological norm | The expert's "5000 disintegrations/min per 150 cm² before washing, natural background after" is stated as the norm *for radiation workers*; he gives no figure for what background on ordinary clothing would read, so "exceeds background" is asserted (Q1: "there should be none at all") but not quantified beyond the preliminary 200-300 counts/min excess. |
| C13 | Tissue radioactivity | All tissue counts above background are 1-7 counts/min on 0.2 g portions; the control heart (3.6·10⁻⁹ Ci/kg) is as "high" as the highest victim sample (Kolevatov heart 3.8·10⁻⁹). The expert's K-40 attribution is consistent with these magnitudes. |
| C14 | Sheets 370-378 | Removed from the file on 10 July 1959 as "not relevant" (L.369 back) and returned before 1996; the radiological documents therefore have a custody gap of ~37 years. |
| C15 | Missing laboratory reports | No histology or chemistry results for Dyatlov, Kolmogorova, Slobodin, Doroshenko, Krivonischenko despite samples being taken (receipt of 10 March, vol. 2 sheet 11). |
| C16 | Name spellings | The Kolmogorova Act alternates «Колмогоровой»/«Колмагоровой»; the Slobodin Act calls the examiner «Возрожденным Б.А.», the 4 March Acts «В.А.»; Laptev's initials appear as «И.И.» and «Ю.И.»; Dubinina's Act has «Иванова Г.Н.» and «28 января». Clerical only. |
