Key structural facts:
- The 1940 AP text and the 1948 Elsevier text are DIFFERENT prose but the same skeleton: SOS first asking for a doctor, then a second message reporting officers dead, rescue party boards, bodies unwounded, second officer on the bridge, explosion(s), fire, sinks next morning "taking her secret". 1940: 13 Nov 1939, 200 nm SE of the Solomons, 12 bodies, finder = American torpedo boat 716 (Il Piccolo/7 Jours) or a British merchant ship (AP). 1948: mid-June 1947, 400 nm SE of the Marshalls, 22 bodies, finder = unnamed ship of the narrator. 1952: Feb 1948, Strait of Malacca, finder unnamed. 1954 (Mielke): 27 June 1947, "Silver Star" and "City of Baltimore".
- The Strait of Malacca first appears in print in the Coast Guard Proceedings (May 1952) unless the American Weekly (Oct 1948) already had it — see sources/reports/us-uk-press_report.md, §3.3.
