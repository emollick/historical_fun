Contents The material was tested chemically with the following results: - (a) Obtained good positive tests for potassium, carbonate, tartrate, chloride and sulphate. (b) Obtained negative tests for resin and calcium. The negative test for calcium is not conclusive proof of its absence, since in the absence of the necessary reagent, the best test could not be applied. (c) The water solution was alkaline to litmus.
