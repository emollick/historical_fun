# Pommersches Urkundenbuch (PUB), Bd. II Abt. 2 (1278–1286), ed. R. Prümers, Stettin 1885 — extracts naming the counts of Spiegelberg

> **Note.** These extracts establish that Counts Nicolaus, Moritz and Hermann of Spiegelberg were at the Pomeranian ducal court from November 1282 to 8 July 1284 (correcting the "† 1283" of the genealogies). They do not mention Hamelin and are not evidence that any Hamelin group travelled with or to them; they show that a channel existed, twelve days after the tradition's date for the exodus.
