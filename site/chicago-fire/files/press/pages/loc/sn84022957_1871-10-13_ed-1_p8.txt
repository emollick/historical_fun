Chicago has been visited with a series
of fires which proved the most destruc-

nent. The first broke out at 11 o’clock
last Saturday night. l
The fire started in a large planing mill
between Clinton, Canal, Van Buren and
Jackson streets. The wind was blowing
very fresh, the flames spread with in-|
credible rapidity, and in a few minutes
the entire structure was a mass of fire.
The immediate vicinity is built up main-
ly with wooden tenement houses and
two-story frame buildings, occupied as
groceries, saloons, etc. The inmates of
many of these houses, started from
slumber, had hardly time to rush out in
their scanty night attire, leaving their
nousehold goods to destruction. In
several instances children were hastily
