To Bo or Not to Be
That is at present the absorbing question
relating to our Market House system. It
involves either the continued existence in
its present form of the Market House sys
tem, or its early abandonment in the hope
of substituting in its stead some more con
venient and available means of accomplish
ing the purposes for which the existing sys
tem was designed. Tho new City .Council
will prubably settle tho question at an early
day, by appropriating money to repair the
old market place, or voting the institution
out of existence. To rebuild or repair the
structure, so as to make it tenable,
might be done without any very disastrous
consequences to the financial condition of
the city, or very seriously discommoding or
troubling any class of our fellow-citizens.
To declare it an unnecessary institution, if
not a public nuisance, and order that it be
swept out of existence, would not necessa
rily result in any very great public afflic
tion. "What now seems absolutely neces
sary is, that one or the other alternatives
mentioned, or some other better plan, be
Immediately resorted to. Something must
be done, and those familiar with the circum
stances of the case fully appreciate the ur
gent necessity for au Immediate change of
some sort.
