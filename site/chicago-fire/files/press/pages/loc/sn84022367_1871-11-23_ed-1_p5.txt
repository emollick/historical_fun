llrandon Graded School.
Editor Rutland Herald -The fall term
of the Brandon Graded School, will close
on the 24th inst., and the winter term
will commence on the 4th of December.
The whole number in attendance dnr-
ing the present term is 378. Of this
number 82 are from out the district, 42 of
whom are in the High School. In the
several departments are pupils from
seventeen different towns, and we hope
that in future the school may have a still
more extended inflaence.
From the first, it has beeu the aim of
teachers to raise the standard, both in
study, instruction, and discipline from
term to term, and from year to year, and
to seme extent we think the object sought
has been gained. The High School
especially, has all things considered, tak
en a higher stand this term than ever be
fore and indeed, in all the departments,
the deportment and progress iu study
have been such as to give great encourage'
ment to teachers, and to all others in
terested in the snocess of the School. At
the commencement of the term, a class of
about twenty-five members of the High
School was formed for daily drill in tbe
branches taught in Common Schools, and
henceforth opportunity f r such drills
will be afforded all connected with the
sahool who desire to prepare for teaohing.
Twelve of the class are to teaoh the com
ing winter. In the elassioal department
there are twenty-six, several of whom are
preparing for college.
Whatever good has already been
achieved is due in part, we think, to per
manence in the board of teaohers, and in
the board of directors, under whese au
thority they act. Five of the teachers
who bean the work of instruction Sept.
7th, 1SC8, still remain, and there has been
no change whatever in the committee.
S j far ss we know the teachers have al
ways been harmonious in their work, and
united in their effjrts to make the school
worthy the support so generally given it
at home, and the patronage so generously
bestowed from abroad, and the commit"
tee have nobly sustained them in their ef
forts, while the people have showed their
interest in the school, by their generous
appropriations for its support.
The school is already supplied with
philosophical and chemical appnratus
fully sufficient to meet present wants, and
assurance is iven that no necessary facil
ity for instruction shall be wanting in the
future. May the school long continue a
blessing to the community, and honor to
the town.
Tbe public examination of the various
departments will commence on the 22J
inst., and continue through the 23i and
24th. Patrons and friends of the school
are earnestly invited to attend.
Attendance upon examinations, and
frequent visits of parents at other times
would much encourage both teachers and
pupils.
Let not the school be forgotten hy those
who desire its prosperity and success.
