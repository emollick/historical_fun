Mr M. M. Ballou, the son of Hosea Ballou,
the great apostle of Universalism in this coun
try, is .about to engage in an important news
paper enterprise in Boston. Mr. Ballou began
newspaper life as one of the publishers ol the
Bay State Democrat, in Boston, more than
thirty years ago. He failed in this, ami went
to writing novelettes for Mr. Gleason, who was
the originator of successful story-papers in
Boston. By and by he bought out Mr. Gleason,
and ran the business in his own name. The
first pictorial paper in America was published
by Mr. Gleason, and afterwards by Mr. Ballou.
Both gentleman made fortunes in the business,
ami Mr. Ballou invested largely in real estate
after he retired, building the St. James hotel as
one of his enterprise*. Now he proposes to re
enter the newspaper field, not with a weekly
pictorial, as has been reported, but with a daily
paper, which shall run in competition with the
Journal, Advertiser, Post, and the rest. Rumor
is to the effect that the new journal is to be on
a large scale in all its departments, and will aim
to do for Boston what the Tribune, Times and
Herald do for New York.
