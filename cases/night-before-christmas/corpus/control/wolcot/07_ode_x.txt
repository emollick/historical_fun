I better works than your's have seen.-—
You'll say, no compliment can well be colder-
Why, as you scarce are in your prime,
And -wait the fbength'ning hand of Time,,
Believe me, BARRET, thou haft truth and taste;
Yet sometimes art thou apt to be uncJiafte.
Too oft thy pencil, or thy genius, flags —
Too oft thy landscapes, bonfires seem to be;
And in thy bustling clouds, methinks I see
The refurreclion of OLD RAGS.
Ah! CATTON, our poor feelings spare!
Suppress thy tram, another year;
Nor of thy folly make us say a hard thing —
And lo! those daubs amongst the many,
Painted by Miller EDWARD FENNY!
They truly are not worth one- half a farthing.
