A BRACE of sinners for no good,
Were order'd to the Virgin Mary's fhritie,
i Who at Loretto dwelt in wax, stone, wood,
And, in a curl'd white wig, look'd wond'rous fine.
Fifty long miles had those sad rogues to travel,
With something in their shoes much worse than gravel;
