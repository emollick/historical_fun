IN the last war French pris'ners often dy'd
Of fevers, colds, and more good things beside:
Presents for valour, from damp' walls and chinks;'
Arid riakednefs, that ieldom fees a shirt;
And vermin, and all forts of dirt;
And multitudes of motley (links,
That might wkh smells of any clime compare,
That ever fought the nose, or fields of air.
As coffins are deem'd neceflary things,
Forming a pretty sort of wooden wings
For wafting men to graves, for t'other world;
Where, anchor'd (doom'd to make no voyages more)
The rudders of our fouls are put ashore,
And all the fails for ever furl'd.
A carpenter, first cousin to the MAY'R,
Hight Mailer SCREW, a man of reputation,
Got leave, through borough int'reft, to prepare
Good wooden lodgings for the Gallic nation;
I mean, for luckless Frenchmen that were dead:
And very well indeed SCREW'S contract sped.
His good friend DEATH made wonderful demands,
As if they play'd into each other's hands;,
As if the Carpenter and DEATH went snacks—
Wishing to make as much as e'er they could,
By this fame contract coffin wood,
Forfuchas DEATH had thrown upon their backs.
