Spare not on brother artists' backs the lash;
Put a good wire in't — let it fla/h;
Since ev'ry 'stroke with int'relt is repaid:
For, though he cannot kill the man outright,
Yet, by this effort of your rival spite,
Fifty to one if ye don't fpoii his trade.
His ruins may be feathers for your nefl;—
The maxim's not s;niss — probatum est.
