title: The Charity Ball
author: George Gordon, Lord Byron
date: c. 1806-1824
source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/27577 (The Works of Lord Byron, Poetry vol. VII, ed. E. H. Coleridge, London: John Murray, 1904)
form: anapestic tetrameter
lines: 9
attribution: certain
notes: jeu d'esprit / minor poem (vol. VII, 1798-1824)

WHAT matter the pangs of a husband and father,
If his sorrows in exile be great or be small,
So the Pharisee's glories around her she gather,
And the saint patronises her "Charity Ball!"

What matters--a heart which, though faulty, was feeling,
Be driven to excesses which once could appal--
That the Sinner should suffer is only fair dealing,
As the Saint keeps her charity back for "the Ball!"

December 10, 1820.
