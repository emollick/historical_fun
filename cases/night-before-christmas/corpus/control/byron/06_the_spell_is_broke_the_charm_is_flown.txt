title: The Spell Is Broke, the Charm Is Flown!
author: George Gordon, Lord Byron
date: c. 1809-1813
source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/21811 (The Works of Lord Byron, Poetry vol. III, ed. E. H. Coleridge, London: John Murray, 1900)
form: iambic tetrameter

attribution: certain
notes: occasional piece (Poems 1809-1813)

The spell is broke, the charm is flown!
Thus is it with Life's fitful fever:
We madly smile when we should groan;
Delirium is our best deceiver.
Each lucid interval of thought
Recalls the woes of Nature's charter;
And He that acts as wise men ought,
But lives--as Saints have died--a martyr.
