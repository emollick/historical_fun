title: Sonnet to George Romney, Esq
author: William Cowper
date: 1792
source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/47790 (The Works of William Cowper, ed. T. S. Grimshawe, London, 1849; shorter poems)
form: iambic pentameter
lines: 14
attribution: certain
notes: 

Romney, expert infallibly to trace
On chart or canvass, not the form alone
And semblance, but however faintly shown,
The mind's impression too on every face--
With strokes that time ought never to erase,
Thou hast so pencill'd mine, that though I own
The subject worthless, I have never known
The artist shining with superior grace.

But this I mark--that symptoms none of woe
In thy incomparable work appear.
Well--I am satisfied it should be so,
Since, on maturer thought, the cause is clear;

For in my looks what sorrow couldst thou see
When I was Hayley's guest, and sat to thee?
