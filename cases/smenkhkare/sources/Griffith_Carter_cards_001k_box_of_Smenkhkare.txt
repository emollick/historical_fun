Card/Transcription No.: 001k-1
1. continued
Wooden box of Smenkh-Ka-Ra
k.	the broken parts of a wooden box with vaulted lid, covered with white
        gesso on exterior and interior surfaces.
Dimensions:  The central rail upon which the names and titles of Akhenaten
and Semenkhkara are inscribed measure  - L. 58; W. 3.5; thickness 1.6 cents.
Description: Lid bears hieratic text in black; knobs bear the cartouche of
Smenkh-Ka-Ra and Nefer-Neferu-aten-mer-na-en Aten; top rail an
incised inscription painted and filled in with greenish blue.
(For texts see further note.)
