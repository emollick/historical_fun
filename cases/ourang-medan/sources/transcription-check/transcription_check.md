# Transcription check of the Ourang Medan report

**Method.** Phase 1: every passage was transcribed blind from the page images (or the PDF text layer, for the two text PDFs) and written to `sources/transcription-check/blind_transcriptions.md` before any report file was opened. Sources not among the page scans supplied for the check were fetched from open sites: Il Piccolo 26 Oct 1940 (dshare-deploy.grupponem.it PDF, saved as `images/pages/ilpiccolo_1940-10-26_p3.pdf`), the Yorkshire Evening Post clipping images (skittishlibrary.co.uk, image-28/29), Revista da Semana 27 May 1950 (archive.org item 1950-maio, page images _0049/_0050 = printed pp. 50–51), De West and Amigoe (resolver.kb.nl article OCR, page ALTO and page JP2 images). Phase 2: comparison with `sources/transcription-check/report_quotations.md` and, for the attribution context of inline quotations, `report/ourang-medan.html`. The crops used are not included in this folder.

**Preliminary finding on the files.** The three De locomotief scans supplied for the check are mislabelled: `locomotief_1948-02-03_p3_full.png` is the 28 Feb 1948 page (header "96e JAARGANG No. 149 … ZATERDAG 28 FEBRUARI 1948"; md5 = images/ddd_010862918_mpeg21_p002_full.png), `locomotief_1948-02-28_p2_full.png` is the 13 Mar 1948 page ("No. 161 … ZATERDAG 13 MAART 1948"; md5 = images/ddd_010862945_mpeg21_p002_full.png) and `locomotief_1948-03-13_p2_full.png` is the 3 Feb 1948 page ("No. 128 … DINSDAG 3 FEBRUARI 1948"). Delpher article OCR confirms: ddd:010862872 = 3 Feb ("Een Mysterie van de Zee"), ddd:010862918 = 28 Feb ("Ondergang der „Ourang Medan""), ddd:010862945 = 13 Mar ("Mysterie der „Ourang Medan""). The figure crops in `report/img/fig_locomotief_*` come from the right pages and the figure captions carry the right identifiers; the report's source list (S14, S16, S18) has the identifiers rotated in the same way as the file names (corrections 9–11 below).

Verdicts: MATCH = same words; MINOR = punctuation, accents, capitalisation, spacing, quotation-mark style; CORRECTION = a word, name, number, date or attribution differs.

## 6. Overall assessment

The report's transcriptions are of high quality. Of 75 passages compared, 55 match word for word, 12 differ only in typography (capitals, quotation-mark style, spacing, a hyphen, an apostrophe), one could not be checked, and seven need correction. The corrections are of three kinds: (i) small silent normalisations inside verbatim Dutch blocks ("Titianic", "groote", the probable "troepentransportschip", the name "Toangi"); (ii) one misreading of sentence boundaries that puts words into a radio message that are not there ("Ik sterf… Ik sterf"); and (iii) errors of attribution rather than transcription: the AP cable's "south-west" quoted as "south-east", the three De locomotief Delpher identifiers and headlines rotated in the source list, the "fourth sentence", and the claim that the Milan reprint carries the same two photographs when it shares only one. The Italian quotations (1940, 1941, the Gazzetta decree), the English quotations (Daily Mirror, China Mail, Yorkshire Evening Post, Proceedings, American Weekly), the Dutch editors' boxes and the Portuguese and Delpher items are accurate; the translations are faithful apart from the two points noted. Nothing found changes the report's argument, but the corrections in section 4 should be applied before publication.
