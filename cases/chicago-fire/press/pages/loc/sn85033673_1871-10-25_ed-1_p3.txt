looked earnestly at him, and in a few
seconds recognized him aa her son, who

ever. He laughingly explained that
since he left her at breakfast he had
purchased an elegant suit of clothes of
Johnston k Vance, the famous and
fashionable clothiers under the Peabody
Hotel. If there is an incredulous person
in the city who disbelieves this tale, let
him go and do likewise, and be convinced
as to the wonderful effect of fine clothes.
