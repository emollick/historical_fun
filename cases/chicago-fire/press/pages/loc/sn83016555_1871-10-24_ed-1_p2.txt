All of our local doctors admit that New
Orleans is suffering under a depression
that retards her development as a great
commercial centre: and each doctor has
a theory of his own about this weakness,
and is prepared with a remedy that will
cure it. Some of these reasons and
avoidances are simply stupid, as, for in
stance, the suggestion that Republicanism
is the root of our trouble, aad that the
return of Democracy will restore us to a
Mazing noon of prosperity. Another is
impracticable—that by varying the in
dustry of the South we shall import
nothing and export everything. Another
is half and half—that all we need is ship
ping, river and ocean, joining the grain
raisers of the West with the grain consum
ers of the East. And like this is another—
that the bar at the mouth of the Missis
sippi only needs to be kept dredged to
afford a channel of twenty feet to give us
