I have been writing a number of
short sketches of distinguished col
ored men for the newspapers of Flo

My object was to incite the color

Among the sketches was one of
Benjamin Banneker, drawn largely

old pamphlets that, have been in

A gentleman of prominence in
this State sent the sketch of Ban
nekor into Kentucky, and it is
fiercely denied that any such man
as Banneker existed, and I am
charged with inventing the sketch
for mere sensational purposes.
Banneker died in 1803, and I
have much concurrent testimony
that such a man as Banneker did
live, and every fact alleged is a veri
table truth, so far as documentary
evidence is concerned. I have seen
in Philadelphia lithograph letters
of the correspondence between
Banneker and Thomas Jefferson
and beside, in Pennsylvania and
Maryland, many literary societies
among the colored people have
borne the honored name of Ban
neker, and exist at the present
time.
I would just as soon think of
denying the existence of Washing
ton, Stephen Girard, Benjamin
Franklin, or Napoleon the First, as
the existence of Banneker; and yet,
if the ordinary concurrent testimony
that such men as ,Washington and
Franklin ever lived was denied, I
might have some trouble to prove
to a mathematical certainty that
Washington and Franklin were
other than historical myths.
Now to place this matter beyond

ilization than the world has yet

so long tiampled him under foot.
and outraged every consideration
