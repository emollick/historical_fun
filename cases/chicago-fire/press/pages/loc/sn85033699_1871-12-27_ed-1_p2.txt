THE DEMOCRACY.
There is no cause for and no reason in
the disorganization of the Democratic par
ty. The Presidential canvass should b-3
conducted on a basis of honor and principle,
not of political bargain and arrangement.
Let the anti-Grant Republicans stay out of
the Grant nominating convention, and thus
avoid the slighest suspicion of committing
themselves to him. Let them advecate
their own honest views and pursue tliat
course which they honestly believe right
and priuciplc require. The Democracy
will do the same. Let those who believe
the present administration to be unfaithful
and corrupt and tending finally to despotism
or anarchy fight it with might and main
Do this and the future will take care of
itself. The progress" of the canvass will
show that those who think alike will have
no difficulty in acting together when the
needed hour for co-operation comes. The
disorganization of the Democracy would not
only weaken it by the lack and loss of discip
line, but would also, if the Democracy be
half so obnoxious to Republicans as those
who favor disorganization would liave us
believe, weaken the anti-Grant Republican
organization.
