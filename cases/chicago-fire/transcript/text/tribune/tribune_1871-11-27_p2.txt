1871 on the 15th of the same month. On the

X mention this, not for any purpose of
self-glorification, for thebe facts are

public, and were frequently commented on
by tho press, but only to show the neces
sity for giving I the official who shall
have the conduct and management of future
sales a certain degree of latitude in regard
thereto. Tho provision requiring tho official
to sell the property in the order in which
the same shall be published seems to me to

heaviest'taxes would be levied, as tho list

suggestion, that a certain optional power bo
given to the Collector with regard to the
sale, that such power would be exceedingly
dangerous in the hands of an nnscrnpulous
