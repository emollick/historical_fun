SERGEANT LOUIS J. LULL
was called and testified, but his testimony con
tained nothing now. Tho fire was finally checked
on Hamsonand Congress streets, between U and
12 o’clock on Monday.
Tho board then took a recess till 2 o’clock.
Tho board again got to work in the afternoon al
2:40 oftlock.
