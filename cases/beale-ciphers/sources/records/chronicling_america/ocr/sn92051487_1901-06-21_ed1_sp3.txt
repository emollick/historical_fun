The manual of summer resorts on
the New York and New Haven road
has just been issued and is a very neat
and attractive pamphlet of 50 pages.
It may be obtained on application to
General Passenger Agent Ilemstead at
New Haven, by enclosing a 2c stamp
for postage. The pamphlet is not a
guide book in a general sense, nor is
it a time table. It is different and
peculiar. It supplies the facts which
persons who travel, and particularly
persons seeking a place of summer
recreation, desire to ascertain about
the living accommodations and their
cost throughout Southern New Eng
land, a region interpenetrated in every
part by the lines of this great railroad
and steamboat system. The informa
tion given is freshly collected and is
as complete and trustworthy as it can
be made by diligent attention and in
quiry. In the matter of prices the
regular tariff of the proprietors of
hotels and boarding houses is given.
No doubt these, in many cases, are
subject to modification by special
agreement according to the greater or
less requirements made. It is unneces
sary to boast of the unrivalled service
of the New York, New Haven Hart
ford Co. 's system. Those who have in
former years been dependent upon it
are aware of its excellent management
for both safety and convenience. No
company serving the traveling public
is more faithfully controlled by a just
sense of its obligations and responsi-
ouities; no company shows more en
terpnse, intelligence and liberality in
doing its proper work. While these
characteristics are apparent to those
who only occasionally use it, they are
of the highest importance to men who,
during the summer season, must place
their families where they can pass
large portion of their own time and
still be in comfortable and prompt
communication with their business
offices. Trains and boats are run with
a frequency and certainty that give
assurance of satisfaction, and they are
of the first class in respect of elegance
and ease.

THE TOWN AND DECIDES ON IM
PROVEMENTS TO BE MADE ON DEPOT

State Highway Commissioner Mac
Donald was in town on Friday last, ar
riving on the train reaching Newtown
at 10.34 a. m., and leaving at 4 p. m
With First Selectman Levi C. Morris
he went over the road between the
Street and the depot. The first ob
jectionable feature that met his eye
was the steep rise, just before the
Street is reached. He told the genial
mayor that this objectionable hill re
minded him of an ugly wart on a man's
nose. It was also on exhibition. His
plans for improving the road are to
cut the hill down to a level grade from
top to bottom, and carting the dirt
and gravel on to the road on the flat.
The doing away with the steep grade
will be a matter for general congratu
lation and felicitation, especially to
those who have heavy teaming. The
surveying for the improvements has
en done, this week, by Daniel G.
Beers. Later, bids will be advertised
for by the selectmen.and the successful
bid must be approved by Commission
er Mac Donald. It is probable work
will not be begun before July 1 or
later.
